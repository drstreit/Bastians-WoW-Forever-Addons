Addons for WoW Forever

Azeroth, told in your own way.

You might be the Wanderer, who explores this new world on their own, guided only by the voices of the land. Or you might be the Scholar, with every quest, creature, item and place one click from its page, its spot on the map and the way there.

Fifteen apps, one install, free software under the GPL-3.0. On CurseForge from the WoW Forever launch, 4 November 2026.

Chapter one

Two ways to walk Azeroth

Same valley, same suite, two moods. The way you pick only decides what is switched on at the start. Nothing is locked, nothing is lost, and you can change your mind at any campfire.

The Wanderer

Explore Azeroth on your own, guided by its voices.

  • The land tells its stories as you walk
  • Talk to people without quest windows
  • Your gestures on a ring, thanks in your own words
  • One key clears the screen for the view

Your first five minutes: hear this place, greet someone, clear the screen.

The Scholar

Every quest, creature, item and place one click away.

  • A page for everything, with where and how
  • The way there, by road, flight and boat
  • Prices, and who of your characters has what
  • A tracker that says what to do here

Your first five minutes: look something up, find the way, know what it is worth.

A welcome that knows who you are

At your first login a window greets you with two pictures of the same valley: you, as the Wanderer, and you, as the Scholar. Your race, that is. A tauren sees a tauren on that ledge, a gnome sees a gnome.

  • Pick a way, and the suite switches itself to match.
  • Three first steps for your way, ticked off by themselves as you do them.
  • A page for roleplayers and a map of where everything lives.
  • It comes back at every login until you say "Don't show again".

Chapter two

The Wanderer's road

No quest helper arrows, no wall of windows. Just you, the road, and a world that talks back. The Wanderer's apps make Azeroth louder and the interface quieter.

The Orbiter

Bastian's Orbiter

A small round companion on your screen. Around it circle the stories within earshot: the zone you stand in, the dungeon you entered, the quest givers nearby with their quests as little moons, bosses, rares and books.

  • Hover it and it opens up; click any bubble and Spoken reads it to you.
  • Quest givers sit in their real direction from you. Turn around, and they move.
  • What you have heard dims, so the new stories come first.
/bv/orbitervoices by Spoken

Conversations without windows

Talk to a quest giver and no grey window slides in. The Orbiter becomes the conversation: their face in the middle, their quests around it, your answers on an arc. You hear the quest, you see the goal, you say Accept or Not now.

The game's own window is one click away, for the days you miss it.

off by default, the Wanderer turns it on

Gestures

Bastian's Gestures

Hold one key and a ring of eight gestures opens at your cursor. Let go over a wedge and your character waves, bows, salutes, or says one of your own lines. Roll the mouse wheel for the next ring.

  • Three rings to start with: gestures, your lines, and the suite's actions.
  • Lines know their audience: %t is your target, plus race, class, zone and more, even in your character's own languages.
  • Someone buffs you, resurrects you or waves at you? A small bubble offers a thank-you. A duel or trade from a stranger? Bow, wave, or decline politely.
/bgestthe ring editor

Campfire

Bastian's Campfire, WoW Forever

In WoW Forever a campfire is where strangers sit together for a minute. Bastian's Campfire makes the most of that minute.

  • A card while a fire is near: what the camp gives, what you could add from your bags, how long until you are warm.
  • A camp ring in Gestures: sit, warm your hands, offer a feature, thank the host, hear the place's story.
  • Share the fire: your group and guild see it on their map for an hour.
  • The planner: every camp feature, what it is worth at your level, and which of your characters can build it.
/bcamp

One key clears the screen

Azeroth is beautiful. The interface is in the way. One key hides everything but your action bars; the same key brings it back. For the sunset over Westfall, the screenshot, the moment.

There is also a gesture for a screenshot without the interface, because of course there is.

Chat

Bastian's Chat, new, off until you try it

The whole chat, rebuilt for people who actually read it.

  • Windows and tabs you define: Trade over here, your guild over there, roleplay in its own tab.
  • The Messenger: your whispers as conversations, one per person, like a phone.
  • A spam filter with a Junk window, so nothing is lost and nothing shouts.
  • Roleplay names from Total RP 3 and MyRolePlay in say and emote lines, emotes in their own colour.
  • Long texts and copying out of chat.
/bchat

Chapter three

The Scholar's desk

Never alt-tab for a wiki again. Every name is a link, every place is on the map, every map pin knows the way there. The Scholar's apps put the whole world into one window, and keep it light.

Browser

Bastian's Browser

A page for everything: NPCs, items, quests, objects, zones and dungeons. Who sells it, who drops it, which quest needs it, where it stands.

  • Lists filtered by place, service, rank and level, nearest first by travel time.
  • Every creature as a 3D model in front of its zone's painting.
  • Dungeon pages with their floor maps and bosses.
  • One search over all of it, from any window.
/bb/bs

Dungeons

Bastian's Browser

Every dungeon has a page: its level, its bosses in order, and its floor maps with the bosses marked. For the dungeons that exist only on WoW Forever, the maps are hand-painted.

  • City of Dalaran, Dalaran Sewers, Excavation Site: Wetlands, Thane's Rest, Lordaeron: maps by Santiago Reyes, used with his kind permission, credited under each map.
  • Click a map to see it large, with the floors as tabs.
  • Each boss opens its own page: what it drops, where it stands.
/bb

Map

Bastian's Map

One seamless map of the world, and every other app draws on it: quests, vendors, trainers, flight masters, gathering spots, your notes, your route.

  • Type into the map's search, and every match with a place gets a Route button.
  • Right-click anything: route there, open its page, leave a note.
  • Pick a style: the terrain itself, Modern, Pencil, Parchment, or Blizzard's own zone paintings joined into one map.
  • The minimap search finds what the minimap can show, and marks a quest's giver.
  • Timers on the minimap: as many stopwatches and countdowns as you like, kept across reloads.
/bmap

Route

Bastian's Route

"Take me to the vendor in Tanaris." Route answers with the fastest way: flight paths, boats, zeppelins, the tram, portals and your hearthstone, each step with its distance and time, a dotted line on the map and an arrow on screen.

  • Home plans the way back to your inn, with every teleport you have ready.
  • Quest round plans one trip through all your open quests and hand-ins.
  • No waypoint addon needed: Route has its own arrow.
/broute

Quests

Bastian's Quests

Your quest log, sorted the way you play: what you can do in this zone, what waits elsewhere, and what is ready to hand in. Plus what to do next at your level, here and in the next zone.

  • Chains and attunements, so you know where a quest leads.
  • What every reward is worth, before you choose.
  • A tracker with buttons for the map and the route, and your quest items one click away.
  • Quest texts read aloud with Spoken.
/bq

Gold

Bastian's Gold, WoW Forever

What things are worth, today. Prices from your own auction house scans and vendor visits, with the market's recent prices before your first scan. Your income and your gold over time, and how to earn more with the time you have.

Unknown prices say "unknown". We never guess a zero.

/bgold

Bastian's Gold Rumours

Bastian's Strategies, WoW Forever

Meet Bastian, treasure hunter, of Goldshire. One day there will be a statue of him in Stormwind's Trade District. Statues cost money, so he is working on that part, and he writes everything down.

  • A plan for gold on every page, with Bastian's drawing and what happened when he tried it.
  • Today's prices on every page, and the places on your map.
  • Pages open as you reach their level or zone.
  • Every traveller who found a plan first is credited, with a link.
/bbookread it on the web

Notes

Pin a note to a place, an NPC, a quest or an item, with a symbol and a colour. Get a reminder when you come near. Share whole lists with your party, guild or a friend.

/bnotes

Alts

Every bag, bank, mailbox and piece of gear of all your characters in one window: your characters on top, one search over everything they hold, one wide list. Need the linen from your bank alt? Ask them by mail in one click.

/balts

Chapter four

The common ground

Wanderer or Scholar, everyone stands on the same ground: one core, one search, daily data, and a handful of helpers that just do their job.

Core

The settings hub, the button at your minimap, one search over everything, and seven looks for every window: from Modern dark and Glass to Parchment and the game's own frames.

/bastians/bs

Helpers

The small everyday chores: sell junk, repair, restock your reagents, take all mail, accept and hand in quests, IDs on tooltips, and warnings worth hearing.

/bhelp

Data, every day

Quests, NPCs, vendors, trainers, items, places, prices and the book's pages, built for WoW Forever and refreshed every day. It installs itself with the suite.

Your language, your look

  • Seven languages: English, German, French, Spanish, Korean, Simplified and Traditional Chinese. The windows, the names and most quests' whole texts follow your game.
  • Seven looks for every window, with your own fonts and colours, saved per character.
  • One row in the AddOn list: the whole suite folds under one header. One right click switches it on or off.
  • Plays well with others: Titan Panel, Bazooka and ElvUI data bars show the apps; other addons can ask the suite for prices, quests and routes.

Our rules

  • Unknown is not zero.A price we do not know says "unknown". Wrong information is worse than none.
  • Every app works alone.Switch off what you do not need; the rest keeps working.
  • Light on your game.Data loads when it is needed, nothing polls in the background.
  • Nothing plays for you.Helpers do the chores you set them, like selling junk. The playing stays yours: no bots, no auto-posting.

Install

With the CurseForge app

  1. Search for Bastian's Suite and install it. Bastian's Data comes with it.
  2. Restart the game fully the first time. A /reload does not find new addons.
  3. Log in and pick your way. The welcome does the rest.

Good company

Spoken gives the Wanderer's world its voices. Questie fills gaps in where NPCs stand. No waypoint addon is needed. Everything works without them, and the hub tells you once what each would add.

Something wrong?

Tell us on GitHub with the addon and, if the game showed one, the Lua error.
