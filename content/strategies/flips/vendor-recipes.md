Kalldan Felmoon stands above the Wailing Caverns and sells thread and dye to leatherworkers. He also keeps two copies each of two Deviate Scale patterns, and when they're sold it takes him three hours to find more. I sat down beside him to wait for the next lot, fell asleep in the sun, and woke up while a druid was paying for all four.

## Why it pays
Some vendors sell a pattern or a recipe in short supply. Our data marks these as limited: the vendor holds one or two, and once they're gone the next ones take an hour or more. The vendor's price is fixed and small. On the auction house the same pattern costs what a leatherworker or an alchemist will pay to skip the ride and the wait, and that's many times more. You don't need the profession to buy one.

The dearest of them is {item:18949}. {npc:4225} in {zone:1457} and {npc:4589} in {zone:1458} sell it for 20s, one at a time, back after an hour, and today it fetches {price:18949}. The bracers have their own page under crafting.

## What you need
Less than a gold for everything on this page and a character of about level 15 to ride across {zone:1413}. The Alliance also gets {item:7361}, which no vendor in our data sells to the Horde.

## How
1. Ride to {npc:5783} at 46, 36 in the Barrens, above the Wailing Caverns. The Horde flies to the Crossroads and rides south-west, the Alliance flies to Ratchet and rides west.
2. Buy both copies of {item:6474} at 5s 50c and both of {item:6475} at 15s. Those are the vendor's prices.
3. Come back after three hours, or whenever the road takes you past.
4. In your capital, look in at the alchemist for {item:5642} at 18s: {npc:3348} in Orgrimmar, {npc:5178} in Ironforge, {npc:4226} in Darnassus.
5. On the Alliance side, {npc:6731} in {zone:1440} sells the Herbalist's Gloves pattern for 18s.
6. Post one of each on the auction house and keep the rest in the bank. When one sells, post the next.

{live}

## What can go wrong
Other people know these vendors. On the beta players stood beside the bracers vendor and waited, and a pattern that comes back once an hour goes to whoever stands closest. Most of these sell a few times a day on a market, so a bank full of them is too many. The beta's prices came from players with beta gold. At launch nobody has money, and the traveller who told me about Kalldan says to hold the dearer patterns for a couple of months.

## Verdict
Small money for riding about, which I do anyway. I stop at Kalldan's every time I pass through the Barrens.
