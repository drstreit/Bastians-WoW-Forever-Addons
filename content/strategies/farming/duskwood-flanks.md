It is dark in Duskwood at noon and the people of Darkshire look at me as if I were late. I've come with three silver, a sharp knife and a plan to sell wolf meat to people who cook. The innkeeper has heard of me from somebody in Goldshire and wants to talk about it. I said the bill would be settled by Friday. There are a great many wolves north of the road, and I've worked out that by the end of the week the statue's left boot is paid for.

## Why it pays
The {npc:565} drops {item:1015} in a little over half of its kills. Cooks need it for {item:12209} at skill 125, so it sells on the auction house at {price:1015} while a vendor pays {vendor:1015}. Look at that price before you leave. The Alliance markets pay several times what the Horde markets pay for the same flank, and on the cheap side the farm is hardly worth the walk.

The spiders in the same woods add {item:3182}, about one kill in twelve. It sells at {price:3182}, a vendor pays {vendor:3182} when the market is asleep, and tailors and leatherworkers have twenty-two recipes that want it.

Two travellers kept their books on this for an hour and came home with about one and a half gold. It's a modest hour, and it needs no recipe and no skill.

## What you need
Level 20 or so, because the wolves are 20 and 21 and the spiders 18 and 19. Bag space, since the flanks stack to 20. No profession at all.

## Where
Wolves and spiders share a long band along the northern edge of the forest, from the western end to the wooded hills north of Darkshire. The Dire Wolf Alphas keep to the middle of the zone, south of that band.

{plate}

## How
1. Alliance characters take the flight path to Darkshire. Walk north to the band.
2. Work from east to west along it, killing the wolves and whatever spider stands in the way.
3. Turn at the western end and work back.
4. Keep every flank and all the silk. Vendor the rest.
5. Sell the flanks in small stacks. Put the silk up at the market price or give it to a vendor.

{live}

## What can go wrong
The {npc:221168} is level 26, and eleven of them stand in the middle of Duskwood. Stay north of them. The flank's price moves a long way inside a month, so one hour here can be worth a third of the last one. Our loot rates come from the Classic data. I lost the sack twice, and the spirit healer says the woods are no darker than anywhere else.

## Verdict
A steady hour for a character of 20 who is in Duskwood for the quests anyway. I've told Nixie it will pay for the boot. She wrote that down and charged me for the ink.
