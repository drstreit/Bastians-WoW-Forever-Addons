The dwarves at Bael'dun are digging again. They were digging the last time I passed through and they'll still be digging when I'm gone. Nobody at Camp Taurajo knows what they're looking for. I asked the innkeeper, but she only wanted to talk about my bill.

## Why it pays
The {npc:3374} and the {npc:3375} are humanoids, so they carry money and cloth. The loot tables give a {item:2592} in one kill out of four, {item:2589} in one out of eight and {item:4306} in one out of twenty. They belong to a quest, and on Forever a quest's mobs come back as fast as people kill them, so the dig fills up again while you work. They don't fear you, they don't net you and they don't fetch their friends from the next camp. I've worked harder for less.

## What you need
Level 21 to fight them. At 23 they stop being a fight and become work, which is what you want. Empty bags, no profession, no group. A tailor or a first aider can use the cloth. Everyone else sells it.

## Where
Southern Barrens, the dig in front of Bael'dun Keep. The Horde flies to Camp Taurajo and rides south. The Alliance has no flight point close by and walks a long way. Stay on the dig. The Soldiers, Riflemen and Officers up at the keep are level 23 to 26, they hit harder and they carry the same loot. I found that out on my first visit, lying on the floor.

{plate}

## How
1. Start at the west end of the dig and work east, then come back along the other side. By the time you're round, the first ones are standing again.
2. Take one or two at a time. Excavators and Foremen carry the same things.
3. Open any chest you pass. The travellers say a chest here holds a green or a blue now and then. I haven't found one yet.
4. When your bags are full, put the cloth on the auction house and sell the grey junk to any vendor.
5. If you're levelling, come back every few levels and grind one level here. Most people level faster on the dig than at the next quest hub, and they leave with a bag of cloth.

{live}

## What can go wrong
Silk drops in one kill out of twenty, so plan around Wool. Five people can share the dig as long as all five kill quickly, because the respawn follows the killing. The keep is the other problem. I walked up there once to see what the riflemen were guarding and spent the next ten minutes as a ghost.

## Verdict
Steady money for a lazy man. I've made more here than anywhere else in the Barrens, though the Barrens never paid me much.
