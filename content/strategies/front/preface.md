My name is Bastian. I've hunted treasure since I was old enough to hold a shovel, and so far I've found a rusty spoon, a boar that didn't want to be found and a great many people who know exactly how to get rich. They are always happy to explain it. Most of them buy the next round, so I listen.

This book is what they told me. Each page is one way to make gold: why it pays, what you need, where to go, what to do, and what went wrong when I tried it. Usually something went wrong. I put that in as well, so you can avoid it.

The plans aren't mine. Every page names the travellers who told them first, most of them on YouTube, with the minute in the video where they say it. Go and listen to them. They explain it better than I do and they did the work.

The numbers come from the auction house. Where a page says what something is worth, it's today's price on your own realm, not a price somebody remembers from last week. I update the book often, and what's new is listed on the first page.

A page opens when you reach its level or first set foot in its zone. I read the Bael'dun page at level 6 and went there anyway. The spirit healer at Camp Taurajo knows me by name now.
