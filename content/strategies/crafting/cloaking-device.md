Ravenholdt Manor stands in the hills of the {zone:1416}, a long ride up from {zone:1424}. I take the wrong path the first time, so I ride most of it twice. The house is full of guards and assassins, and I keep my hands where they can see them. The one I've come for is {npc:6777}, an engineer who sells a single schematic. I buy it, ride back down and find that my Engineering is forty points short of it.

## Why it pays
The {item:7742} costs 24 silver at {npc:6777}, who holds it as limited stock, and otherwise drops in two kills out of a hundred from {npc:7800} in Gnomeregan. The travellers say the device makes you invisible for a short while and that only an engineer can use it, which our data can't check. They also say few engineers bother with the climb, and that the buyers are engineers who want it for PvP and won't fetch the schematic themselves. At the 7-day medians the {item:4397} sold for about three times its parts on the normal realms and for more on the PvP realms. Today it fetches {price:4397}.

## What you need
Engineering 200 for the device and 170 for its four {item:4389}, each made from an {item:3575} and a {item:10558}. Then two {item:1529}, two {item:1705}, two {item:3864} and one {item:7191}. The wiring only drops, and below the cap mostly from the elite machines in Gnomeregan, so most engineers buy it.

## Where
{npc:6777} stands at 86, 80 in the {zone:1416}, at the far northeast corner of the {zone:1424} map. The nearest flight points are Tarren Mill for the Horde and Southshore for the Alliance.

{plate}

## How
1. Reach Engineering 200 before you go. The schematic is no use to you earlier.
2. Fly to Tarren Mill or Southshore and ride up to the manor. If the schematic isn't in the list, come back later.
3. Make the four Gyrochronatoms and buy the gems and the wiring.
4. Make two or three devices and post one at a time. A vendor pays 50 silver for one, so its deposit is not small; the page on how long to list says how to choose the hours.
5. Undercut by a little or not at all. On the beta most markets sold a few a day, Horde PvP the most.

{live}

## What can go wrong
It sells slowly. When I finally had the forty points and made one, it sat on the auction house for two days, and every auction that runs out costs its deposit. The {item:3864} swings hard, and on the normal beta realms its 30-day high was several times its 7-day median, so buy the gems on a quiet day. If the device really needs Engineering to use, the buyers are only engineers, and there are never many of those.

## Verdict
Worth the climb for an engineer at 200 who doesn't need the gold this week. Post one at a time and keep the rest in the bank.
