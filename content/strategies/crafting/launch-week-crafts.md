On the first morning of a new server there's a queue at every trainer in {zone:1455}. I'm in one behind a gnome who wants to learn all the professions at once. Nobody has money or bags yet, and they all want the same few things. A level 5 character with the right profession can make some of them, and this time I worked out which the night before.

## Why it pays
For the first days everyone is level 1 to 15 together. Every mage, priest and warlock wants a wand at level 5, and every blacksmith and engineer needs bars for skill points. The gatherers are low level too, so materials come in slowly. A traveller says the auction house is full of goods at the vendor price for about a day and a half, and that they cost double or more by the third day. After a week or two most players are past level 13 and skill 150, and the prices fall back.

The bronze is the part people miss. One {item:2840} and one {item:3576} smelt into two {item:2841}. Smiths use bronze from skill 95 to 145 for boots, leggings, maces and the silvered pieces, engineers use it for gizmos and bombs, and they all reach that skill in the same few days.

## What you need
For wands, Enchanting. Forever starts you on the {item:247789}, made from wood and a {item:247786}; one {item:10940} gives three motes. At skill 10 a trainer teaches the {item:11287} for 50 copper. It takes one {item:4470}, 38 copper at a general goods vendor, and one {item:10938}. At 70 the {item:11288} takes two essences and can be used from level 13. For bronze, Mining 65 and 2 silver for Smelt Bronze at a mining trainer.

{plate}

## How
1. Train Enchanting on the first day and make practice wands up to 10.
2. Disenchant every green your quests give you. Buy essence only while it costs less than half a wand.
3. Sell wands while the casters are level 5. Today a {item:11287} fetches {price:11287} and a {item:11288} {price:11288}. On Forever a vendor pays only 1s 10c for the lesser one, so sell to players.
4. If you mine, smelt and hold the bronze until the third or fourth day. Two bars at {price:2841} each against {price:2840} plus {price:3576}.
5. Sell the lot before the first week is over.

{live}

## What can go wrong
Enchanters raise their skill by making wands, so the auction house fills with wands made for the skill point and sold for whatever they fetch. Post a few at a time. Tin usually costs more than copper, and on some markets more than the bronze it makes, so do the sum before you smelt. A traveller says he sold twenty copper breastplates on the beta while he stood in town. I made copper belts that week and sold four of them by the end of the month.

## Verdict
Worth doing in the first week if you have Enchanting or Mining anyway. I wouldn't take up either profession for it after the second.
