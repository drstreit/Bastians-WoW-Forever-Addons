It rains in the Wetlands. It rained when I arrived, it rained all week, and the man who sold me lures in Menethil said it had rained since his grandfather's time. I sat on a rock for four days with a pole and a wet blanket. On the second day a box came up on the line. I opened it on the rock. There was leather inside and a bolt of silk, and I sold both in Ironforge the week after and paid for the blanket.

## Why it pays
Two kinds of water pay here. A blackmouth or firefin school gives its fish on most casts, and in our loot tables one cast in twenty brings up a {item:21113}, a {item:21114} or a {item:3820} instead. Floating wreckage pays the other way round: a trunk on three casts in five, the rum or the kelp on one in five each. So I fish every piece of wreckage I see before I touch a school. The fish sells for {price:6358} because the alchemists need it for {item:6370}. The rum sells for {price:21114}. A vendor pays {vendor:21113} for an unopened trunk, so open it. Our data doesn't list what's inside. The traveller found leather and bolts of silk, and five trunks with the kelp and the fish came to about two gold on his auction house.

## What you need
A pole and lures from {npc:3178} in Menethil Harbor, who also sells {item:17062} and {item:6368}. Our data doesn't say which skill the Wetlands want. The traveller who found the trunks came with a skill of about a hundred and left at 140. Bring empty bags, because the trunks don't stack.

## Where
Our data has no spawns for the Wetlands schools, and the traveller doesn't say where along the water he found them. Menethil Harbor is where you start, with the flight point, the fisherman and {npc:1464}'s inn. The coast north of the harbour belongs to the Bluegill murlocs, level 20 to 28.

{plate}

## How
1. Fly to Menethil Harbor and buy lures from Stuart Fleming.
2. Walk the water's edge and watch the surface. Wreckage first, schools second.
3. Open every trunk as soon as it comes up, or the bags fill.
4. When the water goes quiet, move on. Don't wait at an empty spot.
5. Sell the fish, the rum and the trunks' contents on the auction house, and the kelp too if it fetches more than {vendor:3820}.

{live}

## What can go wrong
The schools run out. The traveller fished the Wetlands until the water was empty and then went ten minutes without seeing a single school. Murlocs come out of the sea along the coast, and the Raiders are level 28. The trunk is a lottery, and our data can't tell you the odds.

## Verdict
Come for the wreckage and the rum, and fish the schools while you wait for more wreckage.
