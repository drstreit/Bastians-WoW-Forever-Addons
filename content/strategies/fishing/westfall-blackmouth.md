I don't like water. It's cold, it gets into your boots and things live in it that bite. The south shore of Westfall is the one place I make an exception, because the sand is warm and nobody there wants money from me. I bought a Fishing Pole for 23 copper, sat down near {npc:4305} and caught half a bag of {item:6358} before dark. I had no idea what they were for. I was carrying them to a vendor in Sentinel Hill when an alchemist stopped me in the road and bought the lot.

## Why it pays
A vendor gives {vendor:6358} for an Oily Blackmouth. The auction house gives {price:6358}, because the fish is an alchemist's reagent. Two of them and an {item:3371} make a {item:6370}, which every alchemy trainer teaches at Alchemy 80. Two oils, a {item:3820} and a {item:3372} make a {item:5634}, so each of those potions eats four fish. The loot tables add one more thing. Every blackmouth school in our data gives a Stranglekelp one cast in twenty, and Stranglekelp is the potion's third reagent.

## What you need
{npc:8934} at Sentinel Hill sells the pole, {item:6529} and both kinds of vial. Our data doesn't say which fishing skill the Westfall schools want. The oil needs Alchemy 80. The potion needs Alchemy 150 and {item:5642}, which costs 18 silver and comes a few at a time from {npc:5178} in Ironforge, {npc:4226} in Darnassus and {npc:3348} in Orgrimmar. For cooking, {npc:4305} sells {item:6368} for 4 silver. It needs Cooking 50 and turns {item:6361} into {item:5095}.

## Where
The south-west shore, a walk from the flight point at Sentinel Hill. The murlocs and crawlers on that beach are level 17 and 18, which is more than most of Westfall. The travellers say the Horde has blackmouth water of its own at Ratchet.

{plate}

## How
1. Buy the pole, a stack of Shiny Baubles and some Empty Vials at Sentinel Hill.
2. Fish a blackmouth school when one shows on the water. When it's empty, walk on along the shore.
3. Keep everything that comes up, the Stranglekelp most of all.
4. In town, look at the live box. If two fish and a vial cost less than the oil, make oil.
5. Sell fish, oil or potions on the auction house.

{live}

## What can go wrong
On the second evening a Tidehunter came out of the water behind me and I ran back to Sentinel Hill with the pole still in my hand. The price of the fish swings more from realm to realm than anything else in this chapter, so look at the live box before you give it an evening. The potion recipe sells out, because the vendors hold only a few.

## Verdict
Quiet work by the sea, and it pays as long as the fish goes to the auction house. If you fish here more than twice, learn Alchemy 80.
